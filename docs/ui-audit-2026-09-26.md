# UI audit — 26 September 2026

Scope: live Chrome on localhost, 1280×720 and 390×844. Inspected attract/demo, camera registration, home, song picker, score focus, difficulty, Settings, library and song editor. Read source to verify causes. No product changes or destructive actions performed. Camera feed was black; actual dance, pause/results and two-player layouts were not validated. No automated tests run for this read-only audit.

## Prioritized findings

1. **P1 — Destructive library removal has no confirmation or undo.** `src/components/Library.tsx:83` immediately calls `onForget`; `src/App.tsx:625` invokes `forget`, which deletes the video, analysis, lighting, saved chart and draft (`src/lib/library.ts:411`). The × button looks like a dismiss control and is next to normal song interactions. Verified by source; deliberately not executed. Add confirmation naming the song and affected custom data, or recoverable removal.
2. **P1 — Mobile carousel alternatives are effectively hidden.** At 390×844 the selected song spans x=41–334, neighbours x=-211–40 and x=335–586 before ancestor clipping. Screenshot shows only the selected song. Focus and difficulty similarly show one card and clipped neighbour edges. There are no explicit previous/next buttons or swipe handlers on these screens; instructions emphasize gestures. `src/index.css:3220` sets a 70vw step and `:3237` hides the difficulty navigation hint. Add persistent pointer/touch previous/next controls as on the home carousel; keep keyboard hints available.
3. **P2 — Editor shortcuts stop working after selecting a marker.** Reproduced: click the marker titled “spot · left foot · 0:01.773”, press Delete; marker remains and Save stays disabled. `src/components/SongEditor.tsx:220` returns for every button target, including timeline markers, before Delete, arrows, undo, duplicate and Z/X/C handling. Limit text-entry exclusions to editable inputs; handle marker-button shortcuts explicitly.
4. **P2 — Camera setup needs scrolling at 1280×720.** Its Back to menu button falls below the viewport, while the camera is vertically centered independently. See `.tracking-screen` at `src/index.css:2642` and camera anchoring at `:2713`. Fit setup copy/actions to available height or keep navigation pinned within the viewport. Do not require dance-floor users to scroll for an exit.
5. **P2 — Timeline markers are difficult to select and identify.** Measured spot targets are 20×22px, with densely packed markers and no visible limb lane labels. Accessible names appear as repeated ●/━/C; detailed type/limb/time is only in the title tooltip. `src/components/SongEditor.css:36`, marker rendering in `src/components/SongEditor.tsx`. Provide descriptive accessible names, lane labels, a larger selection hit area and keyboard movement among markers rather than tabbing through all 288 buttons.
6. **P2 — Settings prioritizes diagnostic tooling over ordinary preferences.** At 390×844 the recorder occupies most of the first screen; Gameplay starts near the bottom, with sound and effects further down. `src/components/GameShell.tsx:190` renders ScoringRecorder first. Put Gameplay/Comfort first and group recorder/diagnostics under an expandable advanced section.

## What works

The cream/plum/teal arcade identity is coherent. Selected song cards are distinct and uniform despite long titles. Editor fields reflow without horizontal page overflow, exact timing remains editable, and dirty-close protection exists. Settings has a keyboard focus loop. The attractor has a clear call to action and a working demo.

## Scope limits and suggested order

Fix removal safety and mobile navigation first, then editor shortcuts/targeting and height-aware setup. Finish with Settings hierarchy and visual polish. This is a bounded visual/interaction audit, not a complete accessibility certification or performance profile; no aggregate health score is assigned to unmeasured dimensions.
